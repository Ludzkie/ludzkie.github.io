TLE Accounting: Inventory Systems & Shipping Terms

1. Periodic vs. Perpetual Inventory Systems

Feature Periodic Inventory System Perpetual Inventory System
Tracking Updated only at period-end via physical count. Updated continuously with every sale/purchase.
COGS Calculation Beginning Inv + Net Purchases - Ending Inv Recorded at the moment of every sale.
Inventory Accounts Used Purchases, Freight-In, Purchase Discounts, etc. Merchandise Inventory (directly credited/debited)

2. Shipping Terms Summary

Shipping Term Ownership Transfers At Who Bears Freight? Freight Account Used
FOB Shipping Point Seller's Shipping Dock Buyer Freight-In / Merchandise Inventory
FOB Destination Buyer's Receiving Dock Seller Freight-Out / Delivery Expense

3. Quick Study Cheat Sheet for Shipping Terms

  • SP Collect and Destination Prepaid: Direct Freight — party responsible pays carrier directly upon delivery/shipment.
  • SP Prepaid and Destination Prepaid: Focuses on account billing and reduces cash for the seller upfront (`Cr. Cash`).
  • SP Collect and Destination Collect: Focuses on cash at destination (`Cr. Cash` by buyer) and reduces accounts (Receivable & Payable) to adjust ownership/responsibility.
  • SP Prepaid and Destination Collect: The opposite party actually pays cash to the carrier compared to who should bear the expense, requiring reimbursement/offset entries.

4. Debit Memo & Credit Memo Rules

Document Issued By (Issuance) Received By (Receipt) Accounting Purpose
Debit Memo Buyer Seller Informs the seller that their Accounts Payable has been debited (reduced) due to returns/allowances.
Credit Memo Seller Buyer Informs the buyer that their Accounts Receivable has been credited (reduced) for accepted returns/allowances.
