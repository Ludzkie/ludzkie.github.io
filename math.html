Math Exam Review Notes: Quadrilaterals, Proofs & Triangles

1. Quadrilaterals

Fundamental Geometric Relationships

  • Parallelism (\(\parallel\)): Lines, rays, or segments in the same plane that never intersect.
  • Perpendicularity (\(\perp\)): Lines or segments that intersect to form right angles (\(90^\circ\)).
  • Coplanar Lines: Lines lying within the same flat 2D plane.
  • Skew Lines: Non-coplanar lines that do not intersect and are not parallel (3D space).

Polygons & Quadrilaterals

  • Polygon: A closed plane figure with straight line sides.
    • Interior Angle Sum Formula: \(S = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ\)
  • Quadrilateral: A 4-sided polygon (\(n = 4\)).
    • Interior Angle Sum: \(S = (4 - 2) \times 180^\circ = 360^\circ\).

2. Properties of a Parallelogram

Figure 1: Parallelogram ABCD with bisecting diagonals intersecting at midpoint M
  • Opposite Sides: \(AB \parallel CD\), \(AD \parallel BC\), and \(AB \cong CD\), \(AD \cong BC\).
  • Opposite Angles: \(\angle A \cong \angle C\) and \(\angle B \cong \angle D\).
  • Consecutive Angles: \(m\angle A + m\angle B = 180^\circ\).
  • Diagonals: Bisect each other at midpoint \(M\) (\(AM \cong MC\), \(BM \cong MD\)).

4. Inductive and Deductive Reasoning

  • Inductive Reasoning: Making generalizations based on patterns (creates a conjecture).
  • Deductive Reasoning: Reaching logical conclusions based on definitions, postulates, and theorems.
  • Undefined Terms: Point, Line, Plane.
  • Axioms & Postulates: Rules accepted as true without proof.
  • Theorem: A statement logically proven true.
