Research Exam Review Notes

1. Core Concepts & Definitions

SCAMPER (Ideation & Problem-Solving Framework)

An actionable brainstorming technique used to modify, improve, or innovate an existing product, service, or process.

  • S - Substitute: Replace a material, component, rule, or step with something else.
  • C - Combine: Merge two or more features, ideas, or functions together.
  • A - Adapt: Modify or tweak an existing concept from another context to fit your solution.
  • M - Modify / Magnify / Minify: Change attributes like size, shape, color, scale, frequency, or emphasis.
  • P - Put to Another Use: Repurpose the product/service for a new audience or function.
  • E - Eliminate: Remove unnecessary components, steps, complexity, or features.
  • R - Reverse / Rearrange: Flip the process order, swap roles, or invert the layout.

SWOT Analysis (Strategic Assessment Tool)

A structured framework used to evaluate an organization's, product's, or concept's internal position and external environment.

  • Strengths (Internal): Internal advantages, unique resources, or core capabilities that give an edge.
  • Weaknesses (Internal): Internal limitations, lack of resources, or areas needing improvement.
  • Opportunities (External): External trends, market gaps, or favorable factors to capitalize on.
  • Threats (External): External challenges, competition, or regulations that could cause friction.

Prototyping (Low-Fidelity vs. High-Fidelity)

A prototype is an early, preliminary model of a product built to test concepts, gather feedback, and validate usability before full production.

Feature Low-Fidelity (Lo-Fi) High-Fidelity (Hi-Fi)
Medium Paper sketches, wireframes, cardboard, sticky notes. Figma designs, interactive digital apps, 3D prints, functional builds.
Speed & Cost Fast to build, extremely inexpensive. Slower to build, higher cost.
Purpose Test core user flows, layouts, concepts, and structural ideas early. Test visual design, fine interactions, micro-animations, and usability details.
When to Use Discovery & Concept Phase (ideation). Validation & Refinement Phase (pre-development).

2. Scenario Applications

Scenario 1: Redesigning a Campus Coffee Shop Mobile App

SCAMPER Framework

  • S (Substitute): Replace cash/card payments with automated campus student ID balance auto-deduction.
  • C (Combine): Merge the coffee order queue with a live study-space occupancy tracker in the library.
  • A (Adapt): Adapt the drive-thru pickup notification model for walk-up locker pick-ups on campus.
  • M (Magnify): Magnify the rewards system—offer double points during final exam weeks.
  • P (Put to Another Use): Use the pickup notification system to send campus event announcements.
  • E (Eliminate): Eliminate manual pick-up lines by replacing them with smart code-scanned lockers.
  • R (Rearrange): Rearrange the ordering process—select payment and pickup time before picking drinks.

SWOT Analysis

Category Details
Strengths Direct integration with student IDs; captive student/faculty audience.
Weaknesses High order volume spike during 10-minute class breaks causes app bottlenecks.
Opportunities Partner with local bakeries to sell non-coffee food options in-app.
Threats Popular nearby off-campus coffee shops offering lower prices or better loyalty programs.

Scenario 2: Launching an Eco-Friendly Biodegradable Water Bottle

SCAMPER Framework

  • S (Substitute): Replace standard plastic with plant-derived algae pulp composite.
  • C (Combine): Combine a water bottle with a built-in soluble electrolyte tablet compartment in the cap.
  • A (Adapt): Adapt foldable origami designs so the bottle collapses flat when empty.
  • M (Minify): Minify bottle weight by thinning non-structural walls by 30%.
  • P (Put to Another Use): Repurpose used, empty bottles as plantable seed pods (bottle cap contains seeds).
  • E (Eliminate): Eliminate single-use outer paper packaging and labels by laser-engraving branding directly.
  • R (Reverse): Reverse the disposal model—customers return empty bottles to kiosks for instant deposit refunds.

SWOT Analysis

Category Details
Strengths Zero microplastics; strong alignment with sustainability-conscious consumers.
Weaknesses Shorter shelf life compared to traditional PET plastic bottles.
Opportunities Corporate sponsorships for eco-friendly events, marathons, and universities.
Threats Fluctuations in plant-based raw material supply chains and higher production costs.
